# Follow Up Register

Management owns this index of discoveries that must survive a task or thread. Rows link to evidence and do not authorize implementation. Keep task-local details in the originating closeout.

| ID | Bounded problem and impact | Source evidence | Owner | State | Next trigger / return link | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `FU-001` | `{{problem; key boundary}}` | `{{task, commit, check}}` | `{{role}}` | `untriaged` | `{{when to assess; parent if any}}` | `{{accepted task, deferred condition, rejection, or completion evidence}}` |

Use explicit states such as `untriaged`, `decision_needed`, `scheduled`, `deferred`, `resolved`, and `rejected`. A deferral needs a trigger or milestone review. A resolution needs linked reviewed evidence; removing a row is not a resolution.

Review after task acceptance or owner failure and before major handoffs and closeouts. Route each finding to a bounded task, a decision, an explicit deferral, or a supported rejection.

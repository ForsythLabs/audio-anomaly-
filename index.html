How to Use The FXAA v0.7.07

This tool is a sandbox for visually constructing and previewing complex audio waveforms.

  • Adding Nodes: Click the colored buttons in the left bar.

  • Connections: Drag from output (right) to input (left).

  • Preview: Click a node to see its waveform.

  • Live Audio: Connect to 'Master Output' and click Play.
